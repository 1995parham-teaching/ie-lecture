## Things that go stale every term

Check these before the deck is shown:

- **The semester** on the closing slide, currently "Fall 2026". Note that
  `config.toml` has a `semester` param which the generated title card already
  uses — this slide is a second, hand-written copy of the same fact.
- **The suggested-topics list.** Several are now covered elsewhere in the repo:
  WebAssembly, React and Redux all have their own decks from previous years'
  presentations. Either drop them or point students at the existing deck as a
  baseline to beat.
- The rules: groups of 2, 30–45 minutes, at most 1 extra point on the final
  grade.
